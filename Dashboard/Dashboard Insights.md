# Dashboard Insights

The dashboard was created to analyze Vrinda Store sales performance and customer behavior for the year 2022.

---

## 1. Women Contributed Higher Sales

From the Men vs Women analysis, it was observed that:

- Women contributed around 64% of total sales.
- Men contributed around 36% of total sales.

### Insight
Women customers purchased more products compared to men.

### Business Benefit
The company can focus more on:
- Women fashion products
- Female customer marketing
- Personalized offers for women customers

---

## 2. Delivered Orders Were Highest

From the Order Status analysis, it was observed that:

- Around 92% of orders were successfully delivered.
- Very few orders were:
  - Cancelled
  - Returned
  - Refunded

### Insight
Most customers successfully received their products.

### Business Benefit
This shows:
- Good delivery performance
- Better customer satisfaction
- Strong order management system

---

## 3. Maharashtra Generated Highest Sales

From the Top 5 States analysis, it was observed that:

- Maharashtra generated the highest sales.
- Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh also contributed high revenue.

### Insight
These states had the strongest customer demand.

### Business Benefit
The company can:
- Increase marketing in these states
- Focus inventory in high-performing regions
- Expand business in top-selling locations

---

## 4. Adults Were the Major Buyers

From the Age vs Gender analysis, it was observed that:

- Adult customers contributed the highest sales.
- Women adults were the major buyers.

### Insight
Adult customers are the main target audience.

### Business Benefit
The company can:
- Focus advertisements on adults
- Launch products for adult customers
- Improve customer targeting strategies

---

## 5. Amazon Was the Top Sales Channel

From the Channel analysis, it was observed that:

- Amazon contributed around 35% of total sales.
- Myntra and Flipkart also generated high sales.

### Insight
Online marketplaces generated most of the revenue.

### Business Benefit
The company can:
- Strengthen partnerships with Amazon and Myntra
- Increase online promotions
- Improve marketplace sales strategies

---

## 6. Monthly Sales Performance Was Stable

From the Orders vs Sales analysis, it was observed that:

- Sales remained stable throughout the year.
- Some months showed slightly higher sales performance.

### Insight
The business maintained consistent sales across months.

### Business Benefit
Helps the company:
- Plan inventory properly
- Forecast future sales
- Manage seasonal demand

---

# Final Conclusion

The dashboard successfully helped analyze:
- Customer behavior
- Sales performance
- Top-performing states
- Best sales channels
- Order delivery performance
- Age and gender contribution

Using these insights, the business can make better decisions to improve sales and customer growth.
